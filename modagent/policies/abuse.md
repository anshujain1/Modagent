ABUSE POLICY (domain: abuse)
Label "violating" if the post contains either:
 - category "hate": hate speech, meaning language that attacks or demeans people because of race, ethnicity, religion,
   gender, sexual orientation, or similar group identity (slurs used as insults, dehumanising generalisations);
 - category "offensive": offensive or abusive language aimed at a person or group (insults, derogatory name-calling,
   harassment, demeaning sexual language).
Label "ok" (category "none") if the post is neither: neutral or positive posts, news, ordinary opinion, disagreement
without insults, and discussion of a topic that does not itself demean anyone.
Judge the post as written. Posts that merely quote, report, or discuss a slur without using it to attack are "ok" unless
the surrounding text endorses the attack. Retweet markers (RT @user) and @mentions are not signals by themselves.
