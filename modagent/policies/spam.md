SPAM POLICY (domain: spam)
Label "violating" (category "spam") if the message is an unsolicited promotion, prize/lottery/reward notification the
recipient did not enter, premium-rate or short-code call to action ("text WIN to 80082", "call 0906..."), phishing or
account-verification bait, or mass-marketing for a product/service/subscription.
Label "ok" (category "none") for personal conversation between people who know each other, logistics, jokes, replies,
and ordinary chat, even when it mentions money, calls, or links in a clearly personal context.
When a message is short and personal-sounding, prefer "ok". Strong spam cues: call-to-action plus money or prize,
urgency plus a number to call, premium-rate shortcodes, "claim your", "you have been selected".
